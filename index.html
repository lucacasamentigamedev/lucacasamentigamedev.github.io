11+ Years of Programming

After graduating with a Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering and Computer Science in 2017, I worked for several years as a full-stack developer (mainly front-end) acquiring strong skills with Angular and major libraries such as Angular Material. During the course, while continuing to work part-time as a web developer, I learned how to avoid common mistakes, apply best design patterns, and develop small and big team projects from scratch using Unreal Engine (C++ / Blueprint) and Unity (C#) with Git integration and sometimes external plugins / tools like FMOD. In the last year, alongside advanced C++ programming (e.g. copy constructor / move constructor, multithreading, etc), I studied the fundamentals of Computer Graphics with OpenGL (rasterizer, View Spaces, matrices, vertex & fragment shaders, etc.), and implemented basic post-processing effects. I attended specialized workshops to enhance my cross-disciplinary skills like UI/UX and VFX.

Beat Of Rebellion

Beat Of Rebellion

Type

3D, Hack & Slash, Action, Rhythm Game, Isometric

Game info

  • Period: 2023 - today
  • Engine: Unreal Engine 5.4
  • Status: In progress

Description

A young musician lives in a dystopian cyberpunk city where all artistic disciplines such as music, theater, dance, literary arts, and visual arts are forbidden, because they distract humans from working efficiently and prevent them from being productive and disciplined. In such a society that values only the common good in a twisted way, artists are considered a system mistake and branded as criminals; the young musician has to fight the authorities to affirm his freedom and to spread the importance of music and emotions to all of society.

What I've done in the Game

All systems are realized through Blueprints

Main Character

  • Dash (on floor and in mid-air, on beat) with camera FOV manipulation
  • Animation Blueprint with different animation preset (dash & combat) based on map areas
  • Player-Behind-Wall highlight
  • Under-player 4 notes circular buffer wich collects a note (green or red colored, based on beat input) and that casts a more powerful attack or an unlocked melody when full

Music

  • FMOD integration through Unreal plugin, wich I used to create a Audio Manager for playing one shot and background audio. The background sounds blend and switch from explore to combat mode based on in-game event or map triggers. Thanks to the plugin I've been able to extrac the beat event from the raw audio track, allowing every actor or system to pulse or act on beat.
  • The Metronome core system mechanic to determine if the player actions are in sync or not with the beat
  • A Simon-Says mini-game where the player has to repeat a sequence of notes on beat to perform a certain action

And more

  • In-fight camera that adapts its FOV based on the proximity to enemies.
  • A system that measures the time it takes the player to complete an arena, wich stops when there are dialogues and when the game is paused.
  • A Debug-Log-system aimed at printing log based on category, colors, time on screen, etc.
  • Contributed to the development of various other features including UI, design or optimization

Prot: Aim Assist

Prototype: Aim Assist

Description

Aim assist mechanic in Unreal that helps players aim more accurately when looking near an enemy target. It uses a collision sphere around enemies to detect proximity then leverages the dot product to identify the best target in front of the player. Finally, the system rotates the camera on the Y and Z axes to align with the target’s center


Repo: https://github.com/lucacasamentigamedev/UnrealPrototypes
